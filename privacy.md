# ThePoster – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 12 November 2026_

ThePoster (also called MyPoster) is a private, self-hosted tool its owner uses to schedule and publish their own videos to their own YouTube channels and TikTok account.

## 1. What data ThePoster accesses
When an account is connected, ThePoster receives:

- Basic profile information (name, username, avatar, channel details).
- Access tokens that let it upload and publish the videos you choose.
- Statistics for your channels and for videos published through ThePoster (views, likes, comments, shares, subscriber or follower counts).

## 2. How the data is used
The data is used only to publish the videos you schedule and to show you how those videos perform. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

## 3. Google user data
ThePoster's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is accessed through the YouTube API Services; see the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 4. Where the data is stored
All data is stored on the owner's own computer in a private database. It is not sent anywhere except to the platform you are posting to (YouTube or TikTok).

## 5. How long it is kept
Data is kept while the account remains connected. Disconnecting an account in ThePoster deletes its tokens. You can also revoke access at any time from your [Google account permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions) or your TikTok settings.

## 6. Changes
This policy may be updated from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
